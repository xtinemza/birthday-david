A letter

A few honest words for you.

Happy Birthday to the smartest, sharpest person I know. Every single day, I find myself admiring your mind and the effortless way you continue to teach me new things. But what I love most is that beneath that brilliant intellect is someone so wonderfully genuine. You have this amazing ability to be both endlessly capable and completely cool, bringing a relaxed, grounding energy into everything you do.

Beyond everything you know, I love how funny you are and how you always try your best to make me laugh. You make my life so much easier, especially in the way you step up to help carry the mental load so I never feel like I have to handle it alone.

I hope this year is generous to you. I hope it brings the good versions of everything you've been waiting on. And I hope, somewhere in the middle of it, you remember I am here cheering for you. You are my absolute greatest support, and my biggest hope is that I can always be there for you as much as you are here for me. Have the happiest of birthdays.

— with care, always
